## Post copy (primary)

Today I'm thrilled to share something we built and just launched on Google Play: AI School. 🎓

The world of AI moves fast, and most of us learn it hunched over a screen. We wanted to change that. AI School turns AI into lessons you can listen to: clear, spoken, podcast-style. Learn large language models, AI coding tools, and the APIs that power them, hands-free and eyes-up.

What makes it different:

🎧 Audio-first. Every lesson is rewritten into a clear spoken script you can follow on a walk, a workout, or a commute.

📱 Listen first, then open the full written lesson and code when you have a screen.

🔄 A catalog that grows: new lessons stream in, no app update needed.

Behind it is an AI content pipeline that adapts dense technical tutorials into something you can actually learn by ear.
