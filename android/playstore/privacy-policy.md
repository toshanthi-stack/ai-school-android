# AI School - Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-12_

AI School ("the app") is published by Lilly Tech Systems LLC ("we", "us"). This
policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

## Summary
AI School does not require an account, and the app itself does not collect
personal information about you.

## Information we collect
- **No personal data collected by the app.** The app has no login, no analytics
  SDK, and no advertising SDK. We do not collect your name, email, contacts,
  location, or device identifiers.
- **Lesson content delivery.** The app downloads its lesson catalog and audio
  over the internet (HTTPS). These are ordinary content requests and are not
  used to build a profile of you.
- **In-app browser.** Some lessons let you open the full written lesson on our
  website (lillytechsystems.com) inside an in-app browser. Pages you open there
  are subject to the website's own privacy practices, which may include cookies
  or analytics operated by the website. The app does not read or store that
  information.

## Information we share
We do not sell or share personal information. The app contains no third-party
advertising or tracking SDKs.

## Children
AI School is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children
under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Data security
All network communication uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

## Changes to this policy
If the app later adds features that collect data (for example, advertising or
accounts), we will update this policy and the app's Play Data Safety disclosure
before those features ship.
